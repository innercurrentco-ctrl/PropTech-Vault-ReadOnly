
# Nodeara Ownership Strategy & Equity Planning

## 🔑 Key Rules to Make It Work

1. Use a holding company (e.g., InnaEra) to control the IP and potentially own a share of Nodeara.
2. Keep early hires, influencers, and advisors at single-digit ownership or phantom shares (0.1%–2% typical).
3. Cap the employee stock option pool (ESOP) at 10–15%.
4. Retain 50–60% combined founder equity (personally and through holding company).
5. Be intentional about valuation to limit dilution in early rounds.

## ⚠️ Risks to Watch

1. Raising too much too early at a low valuation — this causes heavy dilution.
2. Giving away equity too loosely without milestones or vesting.
3. Overcommitting ownership to advisors or contributors with limited value.
4. Not having founder protections or IP control via the holding structure.
