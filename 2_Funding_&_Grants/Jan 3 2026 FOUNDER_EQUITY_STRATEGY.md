### 📊 Possible Cap Table Strategy (Initial)

| Stakeholder           | % Ownership |
|-----------------------|-------------|
| **You (Founder)**     | 51%         |
| **Team Option Pool**  | 10%         |
| **Pre-seed Investors**| 10–15%      |
| **Seed Investors**    | 15–20%      |
| **Future Rounds**     | 4–14%       |
| **TOTAL**             | 100%        |

> This structure allows for sustainable growth, team incentivization, and fundraising while protecting your majority ownership.

---

### 🔑 Key Rules to Make It Work

1. **Own 100% before you raise**: Begin by allocating shares *after* incorporation, based on strategic decisions.

2. **Cap pre-seed dilution**: Keep pre-seed equity sales under 15%. For example, raise $500K at $3.5M pre-money valuation = 12.5% sold.

3. **Set the 10% option pool now**: Use this to reward:
   - CTO, Developers
   - Marketing & Growth (e.g., Candy)
   - Advisors / Early Evangelists

4. **Implement vesting schedules**:
   - Standard: 4 years vesting with 1-year cliff.
   - Protects against early departures and guarantees value alignment.

5. **Control seed round dilution**:
   - Aim for $1.5M raise at ~$6M pre-money = 20% or less sold.
   - Strong MVP traction helps increase valuation.

---

### ⚡️ Risks to Watch

- **Low valuation traps**: Don’t raise too early at valuations below $1.5M unless absolutely needed.
- **Team equity over-grants**: Avoid over-distributing equity outside the 10% pool.
- **Undefined roles**: Clarify ownership expectations early to avoid legal headaches later.
- **Non-vesting equity**: Always use vesting contracts.
