## 🎯 If You Want CTO / Technical Co-Founder Energy

### 1️⃣ Y Combinator Co-Founder Matching

[https://www.startupschool.org/cofounder-matching](https://www.startupschool.org/cofounder-matching)

High signal.  
People there are explicitly open to joining early-stage companies.

Approach:

- Short.
    
- Specific.
    
- “I’m validating X in Y space. Looking for technical co-founder with Z stack.”
    

No philosophy.  
No vision essay.  
Just wedge + traction.

---

### 2️⃣ Indie Hackers

[https://www.indiehackers.com](https://www.indiehackers.com)

Post in:

- Looking for co-founder
    
- Product ideas
    
- Growth
    

Don’t pitch.  
Share what you’re testing:  
“Testing a creator-distribution conversion layer in a transaction-driven vertical.”

You’ll attract the right type.

---

### 3️⃣ Hacker News – Who’s Hiring / Who Wants to Be Hired

[https://news.ycombinator.com](https://news.ycombinator.com)

Once a month thread.  
High density of real engineers.  
Blunt culture.  
Serious builders.

## 🧠 Now Here’s The Truth

Events like TechTO and Startup Grind are:

- Networking theatre.
    
- Low commitment.
    
- Social energy heavy.
    
- Low conversion.
    

Builders don’t go there to find co-founders.

They go to:

- Maintain visibility.
    
- Socialize.
    
- Recruit talent.
    
- Or because they’re early and unsure.
    

You need people who are:

- Either bored with their current job.
    
- Or already itching to build.
    
- Or recently exited.
    

Not “casually networking.”

---

## ⚡ Strategic Shift

Stop asking:  
“Who will join me?”

Start asking:  
“Who is already building something adjacent that I can plug into?”

You may not need a CTO immediately.  
You may need a technical partner who wants distribution.

That’s different psychology.
