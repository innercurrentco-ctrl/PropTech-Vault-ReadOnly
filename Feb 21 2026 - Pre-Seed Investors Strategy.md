# Nodeara – Pre-Seed Investor Strategy (Phase A)

## Objective

Raise modest pre-seed capital to:

- Finalize MVP (Live Digital Open House model)
- Recruit Growth/System Architect
- Run 3–5 pilot campaigns
- Build reporting + attribution layer
- Fund 12–18 months disciplined execution

Target Raise: Lean, not bloated.
Enough to prove traction. Not vanity capital.

# Who To Target (Pre-Seed Only)

## 1. Strategic Angels (Primary Target)

Ideal Profile:
- Marketplace founders
- PropTech operators
- Creator economy investors
- Marketing-tech founders
- Early-stage operators who understand two-sided platforms

Why:
They understand:
- Network effects
- Incentive balancing
- Attention economics
- Marketplace liquidity

They won’t obsess over immediate ROI guarantees.
