# Nodeara Strategic Architecture Document
## Creator Economy Vertical – Housing Focus

---

# 1. Core Identity

Nodeara is a **verticalized Creator Economy infrastructure layer**, starting with housing.

We are NOT:
- A listing portal
- A brokerage
- A mortgage broker
- A media production company

We ARE:
- A structured campaign marketplace
- An attention routing layer
- A creator monetization engine
- A housing-intent data infrastructure

Property is Phase 1. The long-term vision is vertical creator liquidity.

---

# 2. Strategic Wedge

## Primary Wedge (Phase 1)
Agent-Creators

Why:
- They control listings
- They already create content
- They already spend on marketing
- They need differentiation

Secondary Wedges (Later):
- Independent housing creators
- Developers
- Renovation influencers
- Everyday micro-promoters

---

# 3. Platform Architecture (MVP)

## Attention Flow

KOL Native Post (TikTok / IG / YouTube)
→ Smart Campaign Link
→ Nodeara Mobile Landing Page
→ Soft Engagement
→ Save-to-Account Gate
→ Personalized Feed

## Core MVP Behavior

Primary Metric: **Save-to-Account Ratio**

Why:
- Signals intent
- Creates owned users
- Enables segmentation
- Builds gravity without transaction dependency

When a user signs up after Rent vs Buy content:

They see:
- Monthly payment comparison
- Estimated buying power

- Rent vs Own slider
- Optional pre-qualification CTA

This validates intent immediately.

# 8. Bot & Fraud Prevention

- SMS verification
- Device fingerprinting
- IP clustering detection
- Reward only verified actions
- No raw click rewards

---

# 9. Governance Principles (3D + 5D Alignment)

- Transparent revenue splits
- Clear performance dashboards
- No opaque algorithm throttling
- No pay-to-suppress exposure
- Creator empowerment model
- Fair take-rate ceiling

Nodeara is the routing layer, not the gatekeeper.

# 12. Strategic Guardrails

Do NOT:
- Become a generic video platform
- Overexpand content without routing logic
- Depend on transaction commission early
- Overbuild AI before behavioral density
- Chase enterprise sponsors too early

Focus on:
- Density within a niche
- Behavioral tagging
- Measurable intent clusters
- Campaign repeatability
