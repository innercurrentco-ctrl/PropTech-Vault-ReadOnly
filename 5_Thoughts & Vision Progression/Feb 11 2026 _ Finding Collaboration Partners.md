## 1. Indie Hackers (Community + Forum)

**What it is:** Asynchronous forum of people actively building and shipping  
**Who you want:** People posting failures, metrics, experiments

🔗 [https://www.indiehackers.com](https://www.indiehackers.com)  
🔗 Best sections:

- [https://www.indiehackers.com/products](https://www.indiehackers.com/products)
    
- [https://www.indiehackers.com/interviews](https://www.indiehackers.com/interviews)
    
- https://www.indiehackers.com/groups/bootstrapping
    

**How to use it**

- Read before posting
    
- DM people who write “this didn’t work because…”
    
- Ignore “how I made $1M” fluff

## 2. X (Twitter) — Builder Side Only

**What it is:** Real-time build logs and thinking-in-public  
**Who you want:** People shipping quietly, not selling courses

🔗 https://x.com/search?q=%23buildinpublic&src=typed_query  
🔗 https://x.com/search?q=%23indiehackers&src=typed_query  
🔗 https://x.com/search?q=solo%20founder&src=typed_query

**How to use it**

- Follow 10–15 builders
    
- Reply with _questions_, not pitches
    
- Post small insights about Nodeara’s problem space

## 3. Gumroad (Creators Who Already Monetize)

**What it is:** A live creator economy with payouts, pricing, ownership  
**Who you want:** $500–$5k/mo creators selling tools, templates, systems

🔗 [https://gumroad.com](https://gumroad.com)  
🔗 Explore:

- [https://gumroad.com/discover](https://gumroad.com/discover)
    
- https://gumroad.com/software
    
- https://gumroad.com/creators
    

**How to use it**

- Click creator profiles
    
- Look for people selling _systems_, not art
    
- Message them about **distribution + tracking**, not Nodeara

## 6. Hacker News (Hard Filtered)

**What it is:** Brutally honest technical discussion  
**Who you want:** People who challenge assumptions

🔗 [https://news.ycombinator.com](https://news.ycombinator.com)  
🔗 Best use:

- Comment sections on “Show HN”
    
- Threads about marketplaces, distribution, trust
    

**How to use it**

- Comment with clarity
    
- Expect pushback (it’s good)
    
- Don’t defend ego
