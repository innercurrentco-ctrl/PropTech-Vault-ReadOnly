## 🧱 THE MODEL: Developer Portals as a Sponsored Hybrid

|Component|Purpose|Strategy|
|---|---|---|
|**Branded Developer Portal**|Feels like a mini-site within your ecosystem|Fulfill sponsorship promise, offer visibility, showcase units|
|**Time-limited listing window**|Limits liability/tech debt and future drop-off|Eg. 6–12 months live, then offer upgrade or roll-off|
|**Premium Agent Access**|Floor plans, pricing, marketing packages|Gate access via user tiers — creates incentive to pay|
|**Lead Capture + AI Chat**|Adds measurable ROI and product “wow”|Offer insights back to developers on listing performance|
|**Links as ‘Sponsored Projects’ (non-ad)**|Subtle placement with native utility|Smart way to scale **international dev ads** without banner fatigue|
|**Upsell Path Later**|Turn expired free builds into paid subscriptions|Use real analytics: “you had 10k views, 400 leads — time to upgrade”|

## 🔄 SPONSOR-LED DISCOUNTED (OR FREE) PORTAL FLOW

1. **Developer agrees to sponsor** (ad package, strategic partnership, etc.)
    
2. You offer **portal setup at deep discount or free**
    
3. Portal lives on:
    
    - `platform.com/dev-projects/project-name`
        
4. Inside portal:
    
    - Unit listings auto-sync
        
    - Media hosted
        
    - Lead form, chatbot, floor plan access
        
    - Premium logic (agent login)
        

✅ **This becomes:**

- A perk for the sponsor
    
- A user utility (browse projects, engage, share)
    
- A retention tool for your affiliate/agent ecosystem
    
- A quiet ad funnel for other developers looking in
