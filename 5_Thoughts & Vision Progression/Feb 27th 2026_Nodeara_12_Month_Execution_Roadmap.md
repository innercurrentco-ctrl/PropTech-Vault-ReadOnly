# Nodeara -- 12-Month Execution Roadmap (Bridge Plan)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Overview

This 12-month bridge plan serves two parallel objectives:

1.  Preserve long-term creator-powered infrastructure vision (5D
    alignment).
2.  Maintain strict financial discipline and operational sustainability
    (3D execution).

This is a liquidity and gravity build strategy --- not a burn-driven
growth sprint.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 0 -- Foundation Lock (Month 0--1)

## Objective

Make the MVP structurally sound before scale attempts.

## Deliverables

-   Save-to-Account fully functional
-   Watchlist retrieval working
-   Basic intent tagging (rule-based)
-   Mobile-first campaign page
-   Unique campaign URLs per KOL
-   Basic analytics dashboard (views → saves → signups)

## Do NOT Build Yet

-   Native video hosting
-   Complex AI systems
-   Enterprise sponsor dashboards
-   Offer/transaction mechanics

## KPI Target

-   5 test campaigns
-   100+ verified account saves
-   Baseline save-to-click ratio established

This phase ensures structural integrity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 1 -- Agent-Creator Liquidity (Month 2--4)

## Objective

Prove repeatable campaign economics.

## Focus Group

10--20 social-native agents.

## Campaign Model

-   \$750--\$1,200 structured campaign
-   Production vendor bundled
-   KOL overlay (or agent as KOL)
-   Save-to-account funnel

## Targets

-   30--50 campaigns run
-   1,000+ cumulative account saves
-   Documented campaign case studies

## Revenue Goal

Operational break-even on campaign orchestration.

This proves marketplace viability.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 2 -- Intent Density & Data Layer (Month 4--6)

## Objective

Build behavioral gravity.

## Build

-   Rent vs Buy tool live
-   Refined intent tagging
-   Basic segmentation engine
-   Email/push notification triggers
-   Rule-based "similar listings" algorithm

## Content Expansion

-   Neighborhood intelligence shorts
-   Investment breakdown videos
-   Mortgage education content

## KPI Targets

-   3,000--5,000 total users
-   20%+ Save-to-account rate
-   15% repeat return rate

Gravity begins here.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 3 -- Sponsor Pilot Layer (Month 6--8)

## Objective

Monetize intent clusters.

## Target Partners

-   Independent mortgage brokers
-   Regional lenders
-   Rate comparison platforms

## Requirements Before Outreach

-   Clear intent segmentation data
-   Campaign case studies
-   Conversion behavior metrics
-   Geographic concentration reports

## Offer Structure

Pilot CPA or revenue-share bundle.

## KPI Target

At least one sponsor pilot secured (\$3K--\$10K).

This validates external monetization.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 4 -- Creator Expansion & Bundling (Month 8--10)

## Objective

Expand beyond agent-creators.

## Add

-   Renovation influencers
-   Finance educators
-   Neighborhood commentators

## Implement

-   Tiered KOL structure (10K / 50K / 100K)
-   Sponsor-video pairing logic
-   Campaign bundles (listing + finance + neighborhood)

## Target

100+ cumulative campaigns run.

## Revenue Goal

Sustainable positive monthly cash flow.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 5 -- Infrastructure Reinforcement (Month 10--12)

## Objective

Prepare for scale without losing control.

## Build

-   Creator performance dashboards
-   Sponsor segmentation dashboards
-   Enhanced fraud detection
-   Phase 1 scheduling integration

## Evaluate

-   Native hosting feasibility
-   Fundraising readiness
-   Strategic partnerships

## Do NOT

Expand geography prematurely.

Win one region first.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Financial Discipline

-   Maintain maximum 6-month runway exposure.
-   Keep agent-funded campaigns as foundational revenue.
-   Sponsors remain upside layer, not base layer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12-Month End State Target

By Month 12:

-   8,000--15,000 registered users
-   2,000+ saved listings
-   100+ campaigns completed
-   1--3 recurring sponsor partners
-   Positive unit economics
-   Recognizable housing creator ecosystem in one city

This represents real gravity --- not vanity growth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Strategic Guardrail

If by Month 6: - Save-to-account ratio \< 10% OR - Agents do not repeat
campaigns

Pause expansion. Fix funnel mechanics.

Distribution without retention is illusion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Bridge Philosophy

3D: - Revenue discipline - Measured growth - No burn fantasy

5D: - Creator empowerment - Transparent splits - Redesigning
coordination

Nodeara must hold both simultaneously.
