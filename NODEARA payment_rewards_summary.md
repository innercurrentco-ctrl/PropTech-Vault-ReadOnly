# 📄 Platform Payment Models & Rewards Summary (InnCurrent / Nodeara)

## 🔹 Overview
This document summarizes the proposed structure for payment plans, reward systems, and monetization logic for the InnCurrent/Nodeara platform, with legal and strategic considerations for real estate-adjacent compliance.

---

## 🔸 1. User Payment Plans

### A. **Pay-As-You-Go (PYG)**
- $5–$15 per action (e.g., listing, boosting, scheduling a showing)
- Ideal for one-off or casual users (e.g., single-property sellers or landlords)
- Triggers soft upsells into Subscription with value prompts

### B. **Monthly Subscription Model**
- $9–$10/month (billed annually = $120/year)
- Includes:
  - Unlimited listings
  - Premium tools (AI suggestions, auto-scheduling, analytics)
  - Ability to earn from referrals/affiliates
  - Participation in reward systems
  - Blue check property verification badge (optional add-on)

---

## 🔸 2. Reward Ecosystem (Marketing-Driven, Not Sale-Tied)

### A. **Listing Reward Pool**
- 50% of the seller's subscription fee is allocated to a "reward pool" for that listing
- Rewards distributed to users who share, amplify, or engage with the listing
- Tracked via platform metrics (shares, click-throughs, inquiries)

### B. **Multiple Reward Sources**
| Source            | Description                                              |
|------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Seller           | 50% of subscription can go into reward pool             |
| Sponsor 1        | Optional add-on from seller (e.g., a preferred sponsor) |
| Platform Sponsor | Automatically matched to listing                        |

### C. **Reward Format Options**
- Platform credits (non-cash loyalty points)
- Gift rewards (e.g., vacations, airline points)
- Optional cash redemption (TBD and subject to terms/compliance)

---

## 🔸 3. Affiliate & Referral Eligibility

### 🎯 Must Be a Subscriber to Earn
- Users must have an active monthly or annual subscription to:
  - Create affiliate links
  - Earn from referral traffic
  - Access listing amplification bonuses

### ⚖️ Legal Safety
- Rewards are **not tied to real estate transactions**
- Platform is **not a brokerage**
- Incentives are for **marketing actions**, not sales commissions

---

## 🔸 4. Sponsor Integration

### A. **Sponsor Roles**
- Fund gift pools or bonus rewards in exchange for visibility
- Appear alongside listings they help fund (tasteful, non-intrusive)
- Target audiences by region or listing category

### B. **Sponsor Types**
- Travel partners (vacations, resorts)
- Real estate-adjacent professionals (lawyers, inspectors)
- Developers and builders

---

## 🔸 5. Agent Participation (Optional, Client-Initiated)

- Sellers with agents can invite them to participate via a shareable link
- Agents can use platform tools (marketing only)
- Agent account use is guided by terms protecting platform IP and neutrality
- YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram integrations to come

---

## 🔸 6. Legal Compliance Highlights
- Clear disclosure that platform is not offering real estate advice
- All user rewards are for marketing engagement only
- No compensation tied to actual property transactions
- System is compliant with RECO and RESPA boundaries

---

## ✨ Closing Note
This dual-track monetization and incentive model allows for fast growth, network-powered distribution, and legally clean scaling — without competing against or imitating brokerages.
