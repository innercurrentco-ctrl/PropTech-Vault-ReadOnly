## PropTech Membership Model — Source Read + Sanity Review

### 📄 Membership Offering Summary
- **Price**: $10/month per property
- **Annual Credit**: $120/year
- **Included Services**:
  - Annual appraisal report (1x/year)
  - Property size assessment + floor plan (by architect)
  - Marketing consultation (free via vetted realtors or stagers in exchange for lead gen)
- **Discounts**:
  - 20% off services for multiple-property users
  - 10% discount with associated law firms + use of annual credit toward legal fees

### 🔁 Credit Usage
- If user **sells via platform**: Credit returned as discount on sale fee
- If user **sells via traditional agent**: Platform refunds yearly credit

### 🎁 Referral System
- **Subscriber Referral**: 1 year free membership per successful signup
- **Deal Referral**: Seller/Landlord can optionally offer reward; platform acts as intermediary (like eBay/PayPal model)
  - Platform charges a small fee for generating and tracking affiliate links
  - Videos used in promotion can be:
    - Hosted on social platforms (free)
    - Hosted in-house (premium, ad-free tier)
  - View tracking system to monitor performance (anti-click-farm measures required)
  - Possibility for **sponsored property videos** with ads, or premium ad-free paid tier

### 📈 User Projections
- **Year 1**: 50,000 users
- **Year 1.5**: 200,000 users
- **Year 2 (Toronto/GTA)**: 1,000,000 users
- **Year 2–3 (Asia)**: 1,000,000 users (HK, SG, MY)
- **Year 2–3 (Europe)**: 1,000,000 users

### ✅ Sanity Checks
- **$120/year x 1M users = $120M annual gross** (ambitious; requires low churn + strong CAC efficiency)
- **Real Costs**:
  - Appraisal: $200–$400 avg
  - Architect floor plan: $300–$800
  - Marketing consult: now offset via partner network
- **Key Assumption**: Only 30–40% users actually claim all services annually

### 🧠 Strategic Layers
- **Referral Credit System**: Credit and reward layers allow alignment across agents, users, and promoters
- **Refund Guarantee**: High-trust trigger (but requires fund reserves)
- **Legal Tie-in**: Turns legal firms into downstream revenue partners
- **Upsell Potential**: Premium tier options for high-end users or ad-free content
- **Sponsored Content Option**: Ads help subsidize property videos for mass visibility

### ⚠️ Risk Flags
- Liability for credit refund (if not carefully bounded)
- High cost of service delivery vs. membership income
- Regulatory differences across Asia/Europe
- User acquisition cost (CAC) likely $50–100 in competitive market
- Click-farm or bot-fraud in referral traffic → needs detection system

### 🔮 Source Read Insight
You’re building a **networked value engine**, not just a listing site. This system rewards real contributions—whether they come from agents, users, or third-party promoters—and decentralizes visibility while maintaining trust. 

- Acting as an **intermediary** (eBay/PayPal model) is aligned, **if and only if** safeguards and transparency are embedded.
- Sponsored content + optional ad-free tier lets users choose their energy.
- Referrals feel clean as long as **value = effort = optionality**.
