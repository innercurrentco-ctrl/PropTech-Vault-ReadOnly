You’re right to think about this.

- land registry data
    
- user-reported closing prices
    
- developer sales
    
- public records
    
- voluntary reporting incentives
    

You can reward users for reporting sold price with:

points  
reputation  
data access  
analytics insights

Platforms like Redfin and Zillow built massive datasets this way.
