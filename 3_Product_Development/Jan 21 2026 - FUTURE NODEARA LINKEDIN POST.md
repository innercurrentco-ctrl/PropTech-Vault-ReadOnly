### What to Know Before You Start Shopping
We’ve seen too many people burned by:
- Over-reliance on agent hype
- Misreading pricing power
- Chasing listings instead of building strategy

Whether or not you’re working with anyone yet, here are filters we suggest all serious buyers use:

### 1. Know Your Role: Be Strategic, Not Passive
Real estate isn’t Uber. You don’t need a driver — you need clarity.
- Get your **mortgage pre-approval** ready
- Identify your **top 3 zones or features**
- Be ready to move at the right moment — not just move around

### 2. What You Should Expect (From Any Pro You Work With)
- Insight on seller psychology, not just listing availability
- Offer strategy that works **before** you’re in a rush
- A system, not a personality

### 3. What You Should Avoid
- Agents who promise exclusives that don’t exist
- 30-showing marathons with no outcome
- Fluff metrics and empty rebates
- Emotional pressure masked as urgency
