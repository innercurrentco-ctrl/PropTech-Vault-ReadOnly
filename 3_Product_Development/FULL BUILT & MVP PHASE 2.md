## ⚠️ Where Complexity Explodes — What to Watch Out For

- **Video storage/streaming & CDN load** — serving many users with video content is resource‑heavy, adds cost and demands robust infrastructure + DevOps.
    
- **AI valuation + predictive analytics** — requires clean data, careful modelling, possibly external APIs or massive historical datasets.
    
- **Affiliate tracking, payments, payouts, legal/financial compliance** — involves complex backend logic, potential regulatory scrutiny.
    
- **Tokenization / Fractional ownership ledger** — this brings in legal, compliance, maybe even securities‑law complexity depending on jurisdiction.
    
- **Scaling & data privacy** — as you grow, security, user data, compliance (especially if you scale globally) become non-trivial.

## 🎯 What to Build _First_ (Phase for Funding & Proof-of-Concept)

Before committing to full build, target a “**Phase 2 – Core Modules + Minimal Viable Product**” that includes:

- Listings (CRUD)
    
- Booking/schedule for viewings or contact requests
    
- Basic user roles (seller, buyer, agent)
    
- Affiliate/refer‑a‑friend logic (simple link or code tracking)
    
- Ad/sponsor space implementation (for early monetization)
    
- Basic payment or deposit logic (optional)
    

This “lean full‑feature MVP” gives you **proof of concept**, lets you test **user demand**, and gives a **tangible deliverable for grants or seed funding** — without overshooting costs or timeline.
