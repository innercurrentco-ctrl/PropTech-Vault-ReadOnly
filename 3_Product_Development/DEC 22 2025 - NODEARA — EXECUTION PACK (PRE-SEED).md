1. ONE-PAGE PRE-SEED NARRATIVE
------------------------------
Nodeara enables property owners to reward everyday people for generating real estate visibility through short-form content.

Instead of value being locked inside brokerages and associations, Nodeara redistributes marketing dollars directly to creators and participants.

Initial focus:

- Creator rewards
- Lead visibility

No brokerage activity.
No commissions.
Pure marketing and attribution.
